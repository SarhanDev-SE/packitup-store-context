PACKAGING SIZING & SELECTION GUIDELINES
================================================================================
1. CAKE & BAKERY BOXES:
   • Sizing Formula: Measure cake board diameter + 1 to 2 inches margin. Height should exceed decorated cake top by at least 1 inch.
   • 1/2 lb to 1 lb Bento Cake: 6x6x4 in or Bento Burger clamshells.
   • 1 lb to 1.5 lb Cake: 8x8x4 or 8x8x5 inches.
   • 2 lb to 2.5 lb Cake: 10x10x4 or 10x10x5 inches.
   • 3 lb to 4 lb Cake: 12x12x4 or 12x12x6 inches.
   • 5 lb+ Two-tier / Tall Cake: 14x14x8 or custom tall cake boxes.
   • Cupcake Boxes: Available in 2, 4, 6, and 12 cavity inserts with clear window.
   • Donut & Brownie Boxes: Available in 2, 4, and 6 piece packs with transparent window display.

2. STAND-UP POUCHES (ZIPLOCK):
   • Materials: Natural Brown Kraft, White Kraft, Frosted Matte, Clear Transparent, and Matte Black.
   • Capacities (based on coffee beans / dry grains):
     - 50g / 100g: 9x14 cm or 10x15 cm
     - 250g (1/4 kg): 12x20 cm or 14x20 cm
     - 500g (1/2 kg): 16x24 cm or 18x26 cm
     - 1000g (1 kg): 20x30 cm
   • Features: Heat-sealable top, easy-tear notch, resealable zip lock, food-grade inner barrier.

3. ECO-FRIENDLY BAGASSE & FOOD CONTAINERS:
   • Sugarcane Bagasse: 100% compostable and biodegradable. Microwave safe, freezer safe, leak and oil resistant.
   • Bagasse Bowls: 500ml, 750ml, 1000ml (available with matching leak-proof PET or Bagasse lids).
   • Bagasse Clamshell Meal Boxes: Single compartment, 2-compartment, and 3-compartment options for meal prep and takeaways.
   • Aluminum Containers: Food-grade aluminum foil containers with poly-laminated board or plastic lids. Ideal for baking, biryani, pasta, and oven reheating.

4. DRINKWARE - CUPS, GLASSES & STRAWS:
   • Hot Coffee Cups: Single wall and ripple double-wall insulating cups in 4 oz (espresso), 8 oz (small latte), 12 oz (regular), 16 oz (large).
   • Cold Beverage Cups: High clarity PET cups in 12 oz, 16 oz, and 20 oz with flat puncture lids or dome lids for milkshakes and frappes.
   • Straws: High-grade paper straws and biodegradable PLA straws (6mm for juice/soft drinks, 8mm/12mm for smoothies and bubble tea).

5. PAPER TAKEAWAY & BOUTIQUE BAGS:
   • Twisted paper handle kraft shopping bags in Brown and White.
   • Flat handle grocery takeaway bags.
   • Bakery display bags with clear vertical or horizontal windows.

6. E-COMMERCE & SHIPPING PACKAGING:
   • Corrugated Mailer Boxes: 3-ply heavy-duty flute E and B boxes. Fold-and-tuck self-locking design. Ideal for apparel, cosmetics, tech, and PR kits.
